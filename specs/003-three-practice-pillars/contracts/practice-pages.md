# Contract: Practice Pillar Pages

## Routes

| Route | Primary practice | Required page content |
|---|---|---|
| `/practicas/corporativo` | Derecho corporativo y negocios | Eight required corporate service topics, detail intro, CTA |
| `/practicas/contratacion-publica` | Contratación pública y controversias contractuales | Before/during/after contract journey, four contract types, CTA |
| `/practicas/tributario` | Derecho tributario y defensa fiscal | Unified DIAN/tax defense, six required specialties, CTA |

## Page response contract

Each route MUST render:

- One canonical H1 matching the primary practice.
- A concise introductory description.
- Concrete service groups and required topics from the specification.
- A visible contact CTA that carries the route's primary practice context.
- Links to the other two primary practice pages.
- A usable header/footer and a descriptive page title/description.

## Home card contract

Each primary card MUST render:

- Public title in the required order.
- Brief description suitable for scanning.
- Concrete service list.
- A detail-page link.
- A consultation link carrying the primary practice context.

Complementary cards MAY use the existing anchor flow, but MUST appear after the three primary cards and use reduced visual emphasis.
